Team packet · Follow a prompt

The prompt

Use your judgment within it.

Work in small groups. Designate one person to have your flowchart or prompt ready to go.

Everyone else works through the customer messages and decides what to do.

The prompt

You answer customers for Brew Diligence, a coffee roaster.

Keep good customers happy and be fair to the business.

Refunds under $30 are fine if the story is believable.

Send anything about safety, legal threats, or a long-time customer who is upset to a person.

Don’t refund the same customer twice in 90 days.
