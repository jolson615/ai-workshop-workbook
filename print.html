Session 4: the support desk

  • Follow a flowchart: half the room follows the flowchart exactly.
  • Follow a prompt: the other half follows a prompt and uses their judgment.
  • In each small group, one person has the flowchart or prompt ready to go, and everyone else handles the tickets.
  • Rules role: has the flowchart or prompt ready and tells the group what it says.
  • Tickets role: sorts each ticket with one action: Refund · Replace · Deny · Cancel subscription · Send to a person.

Follow a prompt: the prompt

The prompt

You answer customers for Brew Diligence, a coffee roaster.

Keep good customers happy and be fair to the business.

Refunds under $30 are fine if the story is believable.

Send anything about safety, legal threats, or a long-time customer who is upset to a person.

Don’t refund the same customer twice in 90 days.
